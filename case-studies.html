01 Can Grow Here, Inc. · 2024–Present

Modular CEA Facility
Design System

Design Project Manager & Research & Design Associate

Building and systems design definition for a first-of-kind modular controlled environment agriculture (CEA) facility. The project required synthesizing inputs across CFD modeling, energy simulation, structural engineering, and growing systems research into a coherent architectural framework — serving as the technical backbone for R&D, investor engagement, and commercialization planning.

Work spanned 3D modeling and drawing packages in SketchUp (primary input for 7+ external R&D teams), comparative benchmarking of facility configurations, supplier procurement, and techno-economic infrastructure modeling for the commercial MVP.

Key outcomes
  • Facility engineered for 90% lower energy OpEx vs. conventional greenhouse
  • 25% higher projected crop yield through optimized spatial design
  • Architectural drawings served as primary input for CFD & energy simulation
  • Supported development of a platform targeting the $1.75B Canadian CEA market
